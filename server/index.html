NR-Scope live PRB grid

Cell scan (nrscan + SIB1)

The B210 can only do one thing at a time: the live view pauses while the scan runs (about 10 s to 3 min depending on the band), then you pick a cell and the viewer restarts on it. 40–60 dB suits a nearby commercial cell; a very high gain can overload the front end and hide cells.
